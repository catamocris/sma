
# Pet Care Mobile Application

A mobile app that helps pet owners take care of their animals by keeping all important information in one place.

## Possible Features

- **Pet profiles** – Add and manage pets with details like name, breed, age, weight, and photo.
- **Health tracking** – Keep track of vaccinations, treatments, and vet visits.
- **Nutrition tracking** – Manage feeding schedules and record meals.
- **Activity tracking** – Track walks, playtime, and other activities.
- **Behavior journal** – Record and monitor pet behavior.
- **Calendar and reminders** – Get reminders for appointments, vaccinations, and daily tasks.
- **Dashboard** – View important information and statistics.
- **Pet-friendly locations** – Find nearby vets, parks, and other pet-friendly places.

## Possible AI Features

- **AI Assistant** – Answer pet care questions and offer personalized suggestions.
- **Behavior and activity analysis** – Identify patterns based on recorded data.
- **Document scanning** – Extract information from veterinary documents using OCR and AI.
- **Personalized recommendations** – Suggest activities and routines based on the pet's profile.
